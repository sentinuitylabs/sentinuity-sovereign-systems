# BaseLine Strength: Programming & Autoregulation

## 1. The Core Philosophy
My training is dictated by readiness (Am I fresh? Feeling too good? Not great? Burnt out?). The goal is not maximum output every session, but consistent progress over time without injury.

## 2. The Rep Scheme Protocol

### A. Core Heavy Work (Primary)
*   **Volume:** 3-5 sets of 5 reps (3-5x5).
*   **Load:** Heavier loads (Relative Intensity: RPE 7-9).
*   **Execution:** Technical mastery is non-negotiable. If form breaks, the set ends.

### B. Backoff Sets (Secondary)
*   **Volume:** 3-5 sets of 10 reps (3-5x10).
*   **Load:** Lighter loads, focus on hypertrophy and work capacity.
*   **The Sliding Scale:** The volume of these backoff sets is strictly dictated by the heavy work.
    *   *High Readiness:* If 5x5 heavy felt easy -> minimal backoff volume needed (Minimum Effective Dose achieved).
    *   *Low Readiness:* If heavy work was cut short (e.g., 3x5) -> increase backoff volume to compensate with safer, lighter work.

## 3. The Minimum Effective Dose (MED)
The objective is to find the sweet spot where adaptation occurs without systemic fatigue. We do not chase volume for volume's sake.
