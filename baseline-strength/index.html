03 / The blueprint

YOUR BASELINE IS THE SYSTEM.

Six principles. One aim: become harder to break and easier to keep moving.

01

Rebuild capacity

Training shaped by years of rehabilitation experience, designed to restore strength without treating pain as background noise.

02

Own your mobility

Mobility and flexibility are trained as usable strength — not an afterthought added at the end.

03

Train for longevity

Progress is judged by what you can sustain, recover from and keep doing well over time.

04

Make it personal

Programming is tailored around your body, history, constraints and goals rather than forcing you into a generic template.

05

Use consultation properly

Coaching begins with context: what hurts, what matters, what you can recover from, and what a realistic path forward looks like.

06

Build the baseline

The goal is not to chase random workouts. It is to create a body with enough strength, control and resilience to support the rest of your life.
